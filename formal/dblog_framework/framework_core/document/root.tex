\title{DBLog Framework Core:\\
  A Source-Side Contract for Bracketed State Capture\\
  Interleaved with a Commit-Ordered Log}
\author{Andreas Andreakis}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
  Machine-checked theorem core for Generalized DBLog. One
  source-side contract --- coordinates denoting nested log prefixes
  with a membership oracle, half-open capture windows, per-unit
  refreshes with per-key existential (bracket-local) validity, and a
  canonical latest-wins replay --- is formalized together with its
  contract-level virtual-cut theorem and the instance work connecting
  it to the published formal study of classic watermarked
  DBLog~\cite{andreakis2026theoretical} (whose
  \emph{DBLog\_Virtual\_Cuts} session~\cite{andreakis2026artifact} is
  imported as a separate session with a shared-session refactoring
  that preserves the published mathematical content) and to write-free
  capture in the style of
  read-only incremental snapshots. The protocol family under study is
  that of DBLog~\cite{andreakis2020dblog}: bracketed full-state
  capture interleaved with a commit-ordered change log.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Vocabulary}
The paper calls obligation O2 \emph{bracket-local validity}, the values a
refresh assigns \emph{read results}, the buffer of the range-merge
discipline the \emph{merged result}, and the coordinate of Corollary~1 a
\emph{shared state witness}. The prose of this development uses those
terms. Its formal identifiers keep the earlier names under which the
proofs were checked, namely \texttt{O2\_honest}, \texttt{honest},
\texttt{wit\_honest}, \texttt{wf\_read\_honest},
\texttt{refresh\_honest\_at\_rec}, \texttt{mixing\_shared\_honesty\_lift},
\texttt{wit\_sync\_memberA\_shared\_honesty}, and
\texttt{wit\_sync\_memberB\_shared\_honesty}. Read \emph{honest} as
\emph{valid} wherever an identifier carries it.
